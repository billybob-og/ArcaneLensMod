Arcane Lens Changelog
=====================

0.1.0
-----
Initial release.
- Magic Lens item with 5 configurable spell slots, cast via scroll-wheel
  selection and a dedicated keybind.
- Inscription Workbench for assigning known spells to a lens.
- Soul Pedestal + Lens Pedestal mana system.
- 5 starter spells: Fireball, Heal, Frost Bolt, Blink, Lightning Strike.
- Ancient Spell Books teach spells permanently; known spells persist
  through death.
- Duplicate-spell buffing: assigning the same spell to multiple slots (or
  accumulating it through merges) buffs its effect and reduces its cost
  instead of listing it twice.
- Arcane Ore + Lens Combiner: combine two lenses, accumulating locked
  spells and scaling mana capacity, up to 5 combines per lens lineage.
- Surface library structure connected via jigsaw to an underground
  dungeon (2-12 rooms) with loot chests.
- 8 advancements tracking pedestal linking, lens crafting, maxing out a
  lens's slots/mana, combining lenses, and learning all starter spells.

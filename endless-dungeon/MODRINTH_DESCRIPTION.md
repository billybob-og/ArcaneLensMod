A genuinely endless dungeon - not a fixed-size structure, but a dimension that keeps growing room by room as you explore it. Requires ArcaneLensMod.

<details>
<summary>V1</summary>

- A shared, persistent dungeon dimension that generates in real time as you walk into unexplored passages - no size cap, no fixed layout, the maze keeps extending for as long as you keep exploring.
- Find a portal structure in the overworld and walk through to enter - each portal randomly sends you to one of a growing pool of dungeon entrances, and the matching return portal on the dungeon side always sends you back to the exact overworld spot you left from.
- Craft a Dungeon Teleporter (8 Portal Shards around a stone brick) for a portable way in and out, no physical portal required - use it to enter, use it again inside to return.
- Portal Shards are a new crafting material found in the dungeon's rarest ("godly") chests.
- The starting floor is a mix of corridors, junctions, loot rooms, a rare watchtower, a library, a hideout, a prison, and a room that looks like it's still being built - a sign the dungeon itself keeps growing.
- Rare drop rooms let you physically fall down a shaft to a deeper floor - no teleport, just gravity - leading to a second floor with its own distinct, older-looking aesthetic and entirely its own room set: bigger junctions, flooded and collapsed tunnels, storage rooms, fight rooms, and a rare tall spire.
- Three tiers of loot (common/rare/godly), each floor with its own spawners and dangers.

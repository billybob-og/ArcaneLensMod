# Faith Altar

![](@arcanelens:faith_altar)

Burn items in soul fire here to earn Faith — see [Faith and the Hunger]($faith_and_hunger). Burning the same item repeatedly gives diminishing returns, discouraging bulk-farming a single item for easy Faith.

This base altar stays craftable and usable by everyone, regardless of which god (if any) you've pledged to. See also the god-specific [Pantheon Faith Altars](@arcanelens:pantheon_altars).

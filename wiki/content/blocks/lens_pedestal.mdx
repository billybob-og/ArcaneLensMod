# Lens Pedestal

![](@arcanelens:lens_pedestal)

Charges a placed [Magic Lens](@arcanelens:magic_lens) automatically, drawing mana from any linked [Soul Pedestal](@arcanelens:soul_pedestal) nearby.

## Linking

Place near a Soul Pedestal and link the two together to start charging.

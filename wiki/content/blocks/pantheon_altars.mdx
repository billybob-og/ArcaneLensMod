# Pantheon Faith Altars

> [!WARNING]
> The Pantheon is still in active development — these altars and their perks may change before release.

God-specific Faith Altars, unlocked one at a time by pledging to that god at the [Pantheon]($pantheon). Each behaves like the base [Faith Altar](@arcanelens:faith_altar) but is tied to your devotion progress with that particular god.

| Altar | God |
|---|---|
| Hunger's Faith Altar | The Hunger |
| Florian's Faith Altar | Florian (Fertility) |
| Aurelia's Faith Altar | Aurelia (War) |
| Quetzera's Faith Altar | Quetzera (Sun) |
| Janus's Faith Altar | Janus (Theater) |

> [!NOTE]
> Quetzera's altar deliberately doesn't light itself up like the others — it reads its own light level to generate Faith, so a self-emitting light source would inflate its own reading regardless of actual sun or torch exposure.

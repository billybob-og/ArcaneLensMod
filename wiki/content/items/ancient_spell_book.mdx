# Ancient Spell Book

![](@arcanelens:ancient_spell_book)

Dungeon loot that teaches your Magic Lens a new spell. Each book grants one of the mod's spells, drawn from the loot pool for the dungeon floor it was found on.

## Obtaining

Found as loot throughout the library dungeon. The Pocket Dimension spell is the one exception — it's a Skill Tree-only unlock and never turns up in a spell book.

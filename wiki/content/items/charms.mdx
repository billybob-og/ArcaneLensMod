# Charms

Accessories worn in a Curios charm slot.

| Charm | Effect |
|---|---|
| Magnet Charm | Pulls nearby dropped items toward you |
| Faith Charm | Passive Faith trickle |
| Mana Orb Charm | Passive Magic Lens mana trickle |
| Burning Charcoal Charm | Permanent fire resistance |
| Antivenom Charm | Cures Poison on contact |
| Preserved Wither Rose Charm | Cures Wither on contact |

Only one of each is ever needed — all charms are single-stack accessories. More charm slots can be worn at once by equipping a [Bracelet](@arcanelens:bracelets).

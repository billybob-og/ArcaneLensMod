# Diamond Engraver

![](@arcanelens:diamond_engraver)

A tool used at the [Inscription Workbench](@arcanelens:inscription_workbench) to duplicate or customize a Magic Lens's spell loadout — reassigning which known spells sit in its configurable slots.

## Obtaining

Crafted directly.

# Florian's Antler

![](@arcanelens:florians_antler)

Florian's relic from the [Trials]($god_challenge_hub). Right-click it to call a lesser Florian to fight beside you.

- The summon lasts about a minute and uses the same gore and charge moves as the boss, with a fraction of his health and damage.
- The Antler is not consumed. It needs around five minutes to recover before it will answer again.
- It has no durability, so it can't wear out, and like every god's relic it's fire-proof and rescued from the void.

## Obtaining

Dropped by defeating Florian in his arena. It has no crafting recipe.

# God Hub Medallion

![](@arcanelens:god_hub_medallion)

Opens the way to the [God Challenge Hub]($god_challenge_hub).

- Right-click anywhere outside the hub to step into it.
- Right-click again from inside to return to exactly where you left, facing the same way.

## Obtaining

A Warped Vessel Shard ringed by eight Gold Ingots. Shards drop from Broken Vessel, so you need at least one victory over it first.

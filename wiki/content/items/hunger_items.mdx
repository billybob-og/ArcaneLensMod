# Hunger Items

Items tied to **The Hunger**, the watching presence that awakens after you defeat The One Who Sleeps. See [Faith and the Hunger]($faith_and_hunger) for the full system.

## Token of the Hunger

Right-click to choose between consuming it for **The Hunger's Boon** or **The Hunger's Bindings**.

## The Hunger's Boon

Right-click to consume — permanently extends how long the Hunger's blessing effects last.

## The Hunger's Bindings

Burned at a Faith Altar instead of used directly — shifts the blessing/burden odds in your favor, with bonus effects at high levels.

## The Hunger's Pact

A reusable key granted by the Hunger Idol. Right-click to open the Skill Tree screen. Not craftable — only obtainable through the Idol's exchange.

## Sun and Moon Ward

Right-click to toggle whether the sky shows the Hunger's changed sun and moon appearance — a reversible, per-player visual toggle.

# Materials

Crafting and currency items used throughout the mod's progression.

## Raw Arcane Material

Mined from [Arcane Ore](@arcanelens:arcane_ore); a diamond-tier-gated resource used in the mod's higher-tier crafting.

## Arcane Ink

A rare currency spent on every node in the Skill Tree. Found as dungeon loot, with an expensive Nether Star recipe as a backup source.

<ProjectRecipe id="arcanelens:arcane_ink"/>

## Warped Catalyst

Consumed together with an [Overload Core](@arcanelens:overload_core) to perform the Overload Ritual, tearing open a portal to the Warped Hollow.

<ProjectRecipe id="arcanelens:warped_catalyst"/>

## Warped Vessel Shard & Warped Attunement Template

Boss drops from Broken Vessel. A Warped Vessel Shard is the "addition" material and a Warped Attunement Template the "template" material for smithing [Sleeping God armor](@arcanelens:sleeping_god_armor) up to its [Vessel-Bound](@arcanelens:vessel_bound_armor) form. The template can be duplicated the same way vanilla Ancient City smithing templates are.

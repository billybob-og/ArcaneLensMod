# A Godly Contract

![](@arcanelens:pack_of_the_gods)

> [!WARNING]
> The Pantheon is still in active development — this item's behavior may change before release.

Lets you swap your current pledge for a different god at the [Pantheon]($pantheon). Consumed only when actually swapping — the free first-time pledge doesn't need one.

> [!NOTE]
> Switching gods resets your devotion progress with the new god back to zero.

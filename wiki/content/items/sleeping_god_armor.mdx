# Sleeping God Armor

Diamond-tier armor, fire-immune, dropped by defeating **The One Who Sleeps**. Grants temporary flight, scaled by your accumulated Faith.

Repaired with Warped Fungus instead of diamonds.

## Upgrading

Can be smithed into its [Vessel-Bound](@arcanelens:vessel_bound_armor) form using drops from Broken Vessel — same base stats, but with an improved flight passive and Poison/Wither immunity.

# Aurelia's Spear

![](@arcanelens:spear)

Aurelia's relic from the [Trials]($god_challenge_hub): a blade a little stronger than a diamond sword, with two extra blocks of melee reach on top of the usual — a spear in all but name.

- Fire-proof, and rescued from the void, like every god's relic.
- The first victory over Aurelia hands it over. Later victories pay out Faith instead, unless the Spear has broken — then the next win replaces it.

## Obtaining

Dropped by defeating Aurelia in her arena. It has no crafting recipe.

# Mask of Janus

![](@arcanelens:theater_helmet)

> [!WARNING]
> The Pantheon is still in active development — this item's abilities may change before release.

A helmet granted through devotion to Janus (Theater) — see [The Pantheon]($pantheon). Deliberately zero-defense; its 10% dodge chance is meant to compensate.

## Abilities

Grants three Faith-fueled active abilities:

- **Stage Clone**
- **Crowd Control**
- **Vanishing Act**

Also grants passive fall-damage immunity and reduced mob aggro.

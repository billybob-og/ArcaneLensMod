# Terminal Upgrades

Installed into a [Living Terminal](@arcanelens:storage_network)'s upgrade slots. Both craft freely and aren't gated behind the Skill Tree.

## Compactor Upgrade

Raises the max stack size for items in the connected storage network. One physical item is required per slot of bonus — stacking isn't allowed here specifically so a single slot can't provide many upgrades' worth of bonus at once.

<ProjectRecipe id="arcanelens:stack_upgrade"/>

## Slot Upgrade

Adds bonus pool slots to the connected storage network. Same one-per-slot rule as the Compactor Upgrade.

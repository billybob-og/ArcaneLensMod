# Vessel-Bound Sleeping God Armor

The upgraded form of [Sleeping God armor](@arcanelens:sleeping_god_armor), smithed using **Warped Vessel Shards** and a **Warped Attunement Template** — both dropped by Broken Vessel.

Same stats as the original set, but:

- Its flight passive no longer has a hard cooldown — the budget recovers passively while grounded instead.
- The full set grants Poison and Wither immunity.

## Obtaining

Requires defeating Broken Vessel at least once for the smithing materials, plus the original [Sleeping God armor](@arcanelens:sleeping_god_armor) pieces to upgrade.

# Vessel Summoning Charm

![](@arcanelens:vessel_summoning_charm)

Re-summons **Broken Vessel** inside its own lair, once you've already found the lair and used its one-time original summon.

## Obtaining

Found once you've completed the Overload Ritual for the first time and located Broken Vessel's lair.

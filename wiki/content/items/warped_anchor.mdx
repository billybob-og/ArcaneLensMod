# Warped Anchor

![](@arcanelens:warped_anchor)

Right-click a [Living Terminal](@arcanelens:storage_network) to bind this item to it, then right-click in open air to re-open that Terminal's storage from anywhere.

## Obtaining

Requires the Warped Attunement Skill Tree unlock, gained after defeating Broken Vessel for the first time.

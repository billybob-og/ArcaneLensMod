# Arcane Lens

A spellcasting mod built around the Magic Lens: learn spells, explore an old library dungeon, take down a sleeping god for its power, and earn the favor (or attention) of something that's begun to watch you.

## Core spellcasting

- **Magic Lens** — the central item; equip up to 5 spells at once, cast and cycle through them with keybinds.
- A **mana system** with capacity that grows as you merge lenses together, up to a 5x merge tier.
- 17 unique spells spanning offense, utility, and support — from Fireball and Chain Lightning to Heal, Blink, and Invisibility.
- **Ancient Spell Books**, found in the dungeon, to learn new spells.
- The **Diamond Engraver** and **Inscription Workbench** for duplicating or customizing a lens's spell loadout.
- A **Lens Combiner** to merge two lenses into one, raising max mana and slot count.

## Mana infrastructure

- **Soul Pedestal** — burns soul sand and items to generate mana.
- **Lens Pedestal** — charges a placed lens automatically, linked to nearby Soul Pedestals.
- **Soul Feeder** — auto-feeds items into a linked Soul Pedestal so you don't have to restock by hand.

## Exploration

- A library with an entrance to a multi-room dungeon full of tunnels, treasure rooms, and dangers.
- A standalone Faith Shrine structure.
- A sunken boss chamber holding **The One Who Sleeps**.

> [!TIP]
> Pick up a copy of the in-game **Arcane Guide** (a Patchouli guide book) — it covers every system in the mod and is always obtainable via crafting, the creative inventory, or `/give`.

<details>
<summary>Spoilers — what comes after the boss</summary>

- **The Faith economy** — burn items at Faith Altars for Faith, with diminishing returns to discourage bulk-farming a single item.
- **The Hunger** — a watching presence that starts after defeating The One Who Sleeps, scaling with Faith and repeat kills, bringing blessings, burdens, and its own currency to spend.
- **The Skill Tree** — unlocked through the Hunger Idol, spending Arcane Ink on nodes like Mana Boost, Cost/Cooldown Reduction, the Pocket Dimension spell, Networked Storage, and the Arcane Assembler.
- **Networked Storage** — Living Chests, Storage Connectors, and Living Terminals for a shared, searchable item network.
- **The Overload Ritual** — push a storage network to capacity to tear open a portal to the Warped Hollow, a whole second dimension with its own boss, **Broken Vessel**.
- **The Pantheon** — pledge your faith to one of five gods (Hunger, Florian, Aurelia, Quetzera, or Janus), each granting its own permanent perk track.

</details>

## Add-ons

- **Endless Dungeon** — a separate add-on that requires Arcane Lens: a truly endless, real-time procedurally-generated dungeon dimension.

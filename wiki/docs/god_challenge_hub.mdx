# The Trials

> [!WARNING]
> The Pantheon is still in active development, and so are the Trials. Only Aurelia and Florian have arenas so far, and details here may change before release.

A dark, flat dimension set aside for the gods' trials, reached with a [God Hub Medallion](@arcanelens:god_hub_medallion). A central hub is ringed by one arena per god, each opened by a Challenge Altar.

## Entering and leaving

Right-click the medallion outside the hub to step in, and right-click it again inside to return to where you left.

## The fights

Use a Challenge Altar and you're taken into that god's arena, with their champion standing a few blocks away, facing you.

| God | Champion | Moves | Relic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aurelia | Aurelia | A wound-up spear thrust up close, and a straight-line charge when you back away | [Aurelia's Spear](@arcanelens:spear) |
| Florian | Florian | A gore that tosses you upward, and a head-down charge | [Florian's Antler](@arcanelens:florians_antler) |

Each champion grows faster and hits harder as it's worn down, across three stages.

## Winning and losing

- **Lose:** the fight is over. The champion fades away, and you respawn as you would after any other death.
- **Win:** you're carried back to the hub's centre. Your first victory over a god gives you their relic; every victory after that gives Faith instead. If a relic has broken, the next win replaces it.

Relics are fire-proof and rescued from the void, so the only way to actually lose one is for it to break from use.

See [The Pantheon]($pantheon) for the gods themselves.

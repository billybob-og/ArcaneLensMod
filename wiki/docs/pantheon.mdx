# The Pantheon

> [!WARNING]
> The Pantheon is still in active development. Perks, balance, and details on this page may change before release.

Pledge your Faith to one of five gods at their own dedicated [Faith Altar](@arcanelens:pantheon_altars), unlocking a permanent, god-specific perk track for as long as you stay devoted.

| God | Domain | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| The Hunger | — | Immunity to food-based ailments, plus lifesteal in combat |
| Florian | Fertility | Bonus max hearts that scale with devotion, plus passive regeneration near nature once fully pledged |
| Aurelia | War | Permanently scaling Strength, plus a Totem-of-Undying-style extra life once fully devoted |
| Quetzera | Sun | Daytime buffs (Night Vision, Glowing, Haste, Speed, scaling Armor) that flip into nighttime debuffs after dark |
| Janus | Theater | Grants the [Mask of Janus](@arcanelens:theater_helmet) and its three active abilities, plus passive fall-damage immunity, dodge chance, and reduced aggro |

## Changing Gods

You can change your mind later — pledging to a different god swaps your active perk track, though your devotion progress with the new god starts fresh. Use [A Godly Contract](@arcanelens:pack_of_the_gods) to make the swap after your first free pledge.
